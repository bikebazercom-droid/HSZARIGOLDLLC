--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1: PRODUCT LISTING & STOCK ENTRY MODULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Product Purchase / Stock Entry Form:
   - Form Fields:
     * `Date` (Defaults to today)
     * `Supplier / Party Name` (Text input or dropdown, e.g., "bablu")
     * `Category` (Gold Jewelry 22K, TT Bar, 995, Silver, etc.)
     * `Gross Weight (g)` (Numeric)
     * `Purchase Rate (AED/g)` (Numeric)
     * `Total Cost (AED)` (Auto-calculated: Gross Weight * Purchase Rate)
     * `Narrative Note` (Optional input, e.g., "100.000 gms 22K recd @ 470/g")

2. Product Listing Table UI:
   - Render all active inventory items and stock entries with columns:
     * `Date` | `Supplier` | `Category` | `Weight (g)` | `Purchase Rate (AED/g)` | `Total Cost (AED)` | `Narrative / Batch Ref` | `Actions`

3. Automated Ledger Posting on Stock Save:
   - Posting Rule:
     * `Metal Credit (+ Weight)` -> Increases Live Gold Balance.
     * `Cash Debit (+ Total Cost)` -> Updates Active Cash Balance / Supplier Liability.

PART 2: DOWNLOADABLE DUAL-LEDGER PDF STATEMENT GENERATOR (`statementExporter.ts`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Layout & Header:
   - Header Title: "GOLD & SILVER ACCOUNT LEDGER STATEMENT"
   - Display Selected Account/Party Name and Statement Period Date.

2. Table Grid Columns (Matching International Bullion Ledger Standard):
   - Col 1: `Date`
   - Col 2: `Ref / Voucher` (Purchase ID, Invoice No, or Receipt Voucher Ref)
   - Col 3: `Type & Narrative` (Transaction Title on main line; Narrative Note on sub-line in subtle text)
   - Col 4: `Cash Debit (AED)`
   - Col 5: `Cash Credit (AED)`
   - Col 6: `Cash Balance (AED)` (Running Cash Balance with DR/CR indicator)
   - Col 7: `Metal Debit (g)`
   - Col 8: `Metal Credit (g)`
   - Col 9: `Metal Balance (g)` (Running Gold Balance with DR/CR indicator)

3. Calculation Logic:
   - Compute row-by-row cumulative running balance for both Cash (AED) and Metal (Grams).
   - Render a bold Grand Total Row at the bottom summarizing:
     * Total Cash Debit & Credit
     * Net Final Cash Balance (AED)
     * Total Metal Debit & Credit
     * Net Final Gold Balance (Grams CR/DR)
