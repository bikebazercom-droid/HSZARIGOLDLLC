FEATURE PRD: WHOLESALE B2B INVOICING & UNFIXED METAL RETURN/SETTLEMENT MODULE
================================================================================

Target Objective:
Implement a dedicated Wholesale (B2B) Invoicing & Unfixed Settlement module under the Invoice section to handle trader sales, unfixed price locking, physical gold returns, and automatic stock plus/minus reconciliation across all statements and dashboards.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INVOICE SECTION NAVIGATION (DUAL MODE):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- In the Invoice menu, provide a clear toggle/tab selector:
  * [Retail Customer Invoice] (Existing standard customer billing).
  * [Wholesale / Trader Invoice] (New B2B trader transaction module).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHOLESALE / TRADER INVOICE FEATURES:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Trader Selection: Select an existing wholesale trader/shop or create a new trader profile on the fly (Name, Phone, Address, TRN).
- Stock Deductions: Items issued to a trader (GOLD 9999, TEN TOLA BAR, Jewelry) MUST immediately deduct from live physical vault stock.
- Unfixed vs Fixed Status:
  * Fixed: Price rate per gram is locked at the time of creation.
  * Unfixed: Metal weight is delivered, but gold rate (AED/g) is kept open for later rate-fixing or physical return.
- Physical Return Handling (Gold Return):
  * Allow returning full or partial physical weight/items (e.g., returning Ten Tola Bar or scrap gold).
  * Returned items must immediately PLUS (+) back into live vault stock under their respective categories.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. LEDGER & RECONCILIATION INTEGRATION:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Automatically sync wholesale invoices and returns with:
  * Trader Ledger (Track Cash Balance AED + Pure Metal Weight Balance in grams).
  * Vault Inventory (Real-time stock increases/decreases).
  * Dashboard Overview & Financial Reports.
  * PDF Statements (Include Wholesale Invoices & Return Vouchers in Trader Statement, Daily Statement, and Vault Reports).
