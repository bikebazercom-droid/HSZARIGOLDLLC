---
name: VAT and business profit
description: User's accounting requirement to separate VAT from dashboard business earnings.
---
VAT must be displayed separately and excluded from business Net Profit.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested that profit reflect business earnings, not tax collected for remittance.

**How to apply:** Dashboard profit uses gross invoiced revenue minus stored invoiced VAT minus exact FIFO COGS. Keep VAT on the same invoice/date/visibility basis as revenue. Do not label VAT on pending invoices as collected cash; distinguish invoiced and collected tax in future reporting.
