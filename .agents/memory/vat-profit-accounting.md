---
name: VAT and business profit
description: User's accounting requirement to separate VAT from dashboard business earnings.
---
VAT must be displayed separately and excluded from business Net Profit.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested that profit reflect business earnings, not tax collected for remittance.

**How to apply:** Dashboard and statement profit use gross invoiced revenue minus stored, item-allocated VAT minus exact FIFO COGS. Keep VAT on the same invoice/date/category/visibility basis as revenue. Distinguish all invoiced VAT (including unpaid invoices) from VAT on paid invoices; do not label the former as collected.
